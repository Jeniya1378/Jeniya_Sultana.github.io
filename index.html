What I work on

Wearable biosignals

Measuring fatigue, cognitive load and physical strain from EEG, EMG and PPG signals collected during realistic construction tasks.

Learning from scarce data

Using generative models such as GANs to augment small, noisy biosignal datasets so models generalize to new workers and conditions.

Human–technology interaction

Evaluating how exoskeletons and other assistive technologies affect both the body and the mind of the people who wear them.

Research translation

NSF I-Corps Entrepreneurial Lead

Through the National Science Foundation's Innovation Corps program, I took our lab's research out of the lab and into conversations with the people who would use it. I led the team's customer discovery, interviewing more than 50 industry stakeholders to test whether the problems we study match the problems they face.
